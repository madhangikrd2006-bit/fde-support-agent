# Employee Onboarding Guide — Acme Robotics

## Time Off Policy
New hires accrue paid time off starting on their first day. Full-time employees
receive 18 days of vacation per year during their first two years of employment,
increasing to 23 days after two years of tenure. Vacation requests longer than
five consecutive days require two weeks' notice to your manager.

Sick leave is separate from vacation and is unlimited for documented illness,
though absences longer than three consecutive days require a note from a
healthcare provider.

## Equipment
New hires are issued a laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad, employee's choice) and
a monitor during their first week. IT tickets for hardware issues should be
filed through the #it-help Slack channel, not email.

## Expense Reports
Client meals and entertainment up to $150 per person do not require pre-approval
and can be submitted directly through Expensify. Expenses over $150 per person,
or any single expense over $1,000, require written approval from your manager
before the expense is incurred, not after.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week without approval. Fully
remote arrangements require director-level sign-off and are evaluated on a
role-by-role basis.
