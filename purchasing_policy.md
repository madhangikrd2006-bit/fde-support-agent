# Purchasing & Procurement Policy — Acme Robotics

## Approval Thresholds
- Purchases under $1,000: may be approved by the requester's direct manager.
- Purchases between $1,000 and $5,000: require director approval in addition
  to manager sign-off.
- Purchases over $5,000: require approval from the Finance Director and must
  be accompanied by at least two competing vendor quotes.
- Purchases over $50,000 require CFO approval and a signed contract reviewed
  by Legal.

## Preferred Vendors
Engineering hardware should be purchased through the approved vendor list
maintained by IT Procurement. Purchasing outside the approved list requires
a documented exception, filed via the Procurement Exception form.

## Software Subscriptions
New SaaS subscriptions over $200/month must be reviewed by Security before
purchase, regardless of the approval threshold above, due to data-handling
requirements. Security review typically takes 3-5 business days.

## Reimbursement Timing
Approved reimbursements are processed on the next bi-weekly payroll cycle
after submission. Submissions made after the 25th of the month may roll to
the following cycle.
